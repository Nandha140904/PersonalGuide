Description (4000 chars max):
---
Personal Guide is your quiet, focused life-administration engine — built entirely on your iPhone with no account required, no cloud sync, and zero tracking.

WHAT IT DOES

Life admin is exhausting. Chasing insurance renewals, tracking returns, managing document deadlines, keeping on top of bills — it all lives in your head, in scattered emails, and half-finished notes. Personal Guide gives every open task a structured workspace with clear next steps, so nothing gets dropped.

CORE FEATURES

• Case Workspaces — Every life-admin task gets its own mini-workspace. See what you know, what you need, and exactly what to do next.
• Document Scanning — Point your camera at any document: receipt, bill, policy, contract. On-device OCR extracts the key facts instantly.
• Smart Action Plans — Personal Guide generates ordered action checklists based on the type of case (return window, renewal deadline, claim process).
• Deadline Intelligence — The priority engine surfaces what needs attention today, this week, and coming up — so you always know what to focus on.
• Asset Registry — Track your real-world belongings (car, phone, laptop, appliances) with warranty expiry dates and linked insurance cases.
• Live Search — Search across all your cases, documents (including scanned text), and assets in real time.
• Reminder Alerts — Receive actionable deadline reminders at 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 day before key dates.
• Face ID Protection — Add a biometric lock to keep your documents private from anyone who picks up your phone.
• JSON Export — Export a full structured backup of your data at any time.
• Complete Data Wipe — Remove every record with one action and full confirmation.

PRIVACY FIRST

Every feature runs 100% on-device. Personal Guide uses Apple Vision for OCR, Apple's NaturalLanguage framework for understanding, and SwiftData for local persistence. No account, no login, no analytics, no ads. Your data is yours.

Built with Swift 6, SwiftData, and SwiftUI — the same frameworks Apple engineers use to build iOS itself.

# REVIEW NOTES (for App Review team)
# ============================================================

Demo Account:
  Not required. The app has no accounts or login.

Special Instructions:
  The app uses Face ID for optional biometric lock (NSFaceIDUsageDescription is set).
  The app uses the camera for document scanning (NSCameraUsageDescription is set).
  Both permissions can be demonstrated in the simulator with the simulator's built-in camera simulation.
  All data is stored 100% locally; no network requests are made except by the optional cloud AI providers
  (Google Gemini / OpenAI), which the user must manually configure with their own API key in the AI Engine settings.
